Qigong

Morning Routine

An eight-move qigong sequence for the morning — beginner-friendly, no equipment, 10-15 minutes.

1. Waking the Dragon

Start at the feet. Shake gently. Let the shake travel up through the ankles, knees, hips, spine, shoulders, and out through the hands. 30-60 seconds. Loosens the whole body from sleep. Sets the tone: the day begins by moving, not by thinking.

2. Pulling Down Heaven

Stand with feet shoulder-width. Inhale and raise both arms overhead as if gathering something from above. Exhale and press the palms slowly down in front of the body, from crown to belly. Repeat 6-9 times. Grounds scattered morning energy. Connects breath to movement.

3. Opening the Chest

Arms extend forward at shoulder height. On the inhale, sweep the arms wide and back, opening the chest fully. On the exhale, bring them back together. Repeat 6-9 times. Counters the closed posture of sleep and screen time. Literally makes room to breathe.

4. Dragon Looks at Its Tail

Stand relaxed. Turn the head and upper body slowly to the left, looking back over the shoulder as far as comfortable. Hold one breath. Return to center. Repeat right. Do 3-5 each side. Releases the cervical spine and upper back. Reactivates peripheral vision, which settles the nervous system.

5. Cloud Hands

Shift weight slowly from left foot to right, arms floating in a slow horizontal figure-eight pattern in front of the body. Move as if in slow water. 1-2 minutes. A moving meditation. The lateral weight shift and slow pace drop heart rate and quiet the mind.

6. Kidney Knocking

Make loose fists. Reach both hands behind you and gently knock the area of the lower back where the kidneys sit, with alternating light taps. 30-60 seconds. Warms the core and lower back. In traditional qigong, stimulates kidney energy, associated with willpower, fear, and vitality.

7. Embrace the Tree (Zhan Zhuang)

Stand with feet shoulder-width, knees very slightly bent. Raise both arms to chest height, rounded, as if holding a large ball. Relax the shoulders. Hold for 1-5 minutes, breathing naturally. A standing meditation. Builds body awareness, settles the nervous system, develops quiet strength without strain.

8. Returning to Earth

Inhale and rise onto the balls of the feet, arms floating out to the sides. Exhale and lower the heels back to the ground, pressing the palms slowly down. Repeat 6-9 times. The closing move. Grounds the energy built during the practice. A conscious return to the earth before the day begins.

Additional Moves

For variety, or a longer session.

Swinging Arms

Swing both arms left and right across the body, letting them wrap loosely around the torso with each swing. Let the momentum do the work. 1-2 minutes. Loosens the shoulders, spine, and hips. Good warmup before the main sequence or as a standalone reset.

Spinal Ripple

Stand relaxed, feet shoulder-width. Begin a slow wave motion from the crown of the head, letting it travel down through the cervical spine, thoracic spine, lumbar, and tail. Reverse it. Move like seaweed in a gentle current. 1 minute. Decompresses the spine. Restores natural spinal movement that daily sitting removes.

Still Water Standing

Stand quietly with no movement and no goal. Eyes soft or closed. One minute, then two, then longer. Simply notice. This is not a warm-up and not a cooldown. It is the practice itself — the ability to stand still and be undisturbed. Many people cannot do this for 60 seconds when they begin. It gets easier.

The Silk Thread

Imagine a single thread of silk attached to the crown of the head, gently lifting. Let the spine lengthen, the shoulders drop, the chin tuck slightly. Hold this posture while breathing slowly for 1-2 minutes. Restores posture without effort. The silk thread cue works where "stand up straight" fails.

Shaking Out the Hands

Hang the arms at the sides and shake the hands loosely from the wrists. Let the fingers flop. 30 seconds. Simple and underrated. Releases tension that accumulates in the hands, wrists, and forearms, especially for anyone who types or uses a phone. Good standalone reset mid-day.
