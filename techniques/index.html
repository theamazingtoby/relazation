Right Now · Every Day

Short, specific practices — what to reach for in the moment, and what to build over time.

Right Now

In the moment — for when you're in acute stress or anxiety, right now.

Physiological SighDoubles CO2 clearance in one breath. Activates the vagus nerve. Fastest known reset for acute stress. ValsalvaIncreases vagal tone directly by raising chest pressure, slowing the heart rate fast. Extended ExhaleA longer exhale than inhale shifts the nervous system from sympathetic to parasympathetic dominance. Lateral GazeBilateral eye movement is thought to help the brain process and settle stuck stress responses. HummingHumming vibrates the vagus nerve through the vocal cords, boosting nitric oxide and vagal tone. Grounding (5-4-3-2-1)Directs attention outward through the five senses, interrupting the internal anxious narrative.
Every Day

Ongoing practice — build capacity over time, not just in a crisis.

Body ScanShifts attention from anxious thought loops into present-moment bodily sensation, lowering arousal. Resonance BreathingBreathing at roughly 6 breaths per minute maximizes heart rate variability, the body's resilience marker. 4-7-8 BreathingThe long hold and even-longer exhale push the nervous system toward calm faster than normal breathing. Cold Water / Dive ReflexTriggers the mammalian dive reflex. Slows heart rate, redirects blood to core organs. Panoramic VisionWide-angle vision signals safety to the brainstem, the opposite of the narrowed tunnel vision of threat. Nature WalkCombines movement, panoramic vision, and reduced sensory load, three regulators at once. Cold Shower / Cold PlungeTriggers a norepinephrine spike and parasympathetic rebound. Trains the nervous system to recover from stress. JournalingExternalizing a thought reduces its grip. The brain stops rehearsing what's already been recorded.
