Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

Relazation (relazation.com) is a personal, independently-run site. This page explains what data is collected when you visit or use it.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages and content get read or watched. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard web-analytics data (pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type, referring site). It does not collect your name or contact details. You can opt out using a browser extension like Google's own Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by using your browser's tracking-protection settings.

Email subscription

If you subscribe to the newsletter, that signup is handled entirely by Substack, not by this site directly. Your email address is stored and managed under Substack's own privacy policy. Relazation only sees your subscription status and whatever you choose to reply with; it doesn't otherwise sell, share, or use your email address for anything beyond the newsletter itself.

Purchases

Any purchases (technique card decks, downloads) are processed entirely by Gumroad, not by this site. Payment details are never seen or stored by Relazation — see Gumroad's privacy policy for how they handle your information.

Embedded content

Some pages embed videos or audio from YouTube, SoundCloud, or similar platforms. Playing an embed may let that platform set its own cookies or collect data according to its own privacy policy — this happens between you and that platform, not through Relazation.

What Relazation itself does not do

This site does not run its own account/login system, does not store payment information, and does not sell or share visitor data with third parties beyond the standard operation of the services listed above (Google Analytics, Substack, Gumroad, and video/audio embeds).

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via any of the contact links in the footer, or through the Substack page.
