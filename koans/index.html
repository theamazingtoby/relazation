Koans

Sit With This

A koan is not a riddle. It is not meant to be solved. It is meant to be sat with until something shifts.

Original Relazation Koans
  • If calm is what you are looking for, where will you find it when you stop looking?
  • The wave worries about becoming the shore. What does the ocean think of this?
  • You say you want peace. What would you do if it arrived?
  • If anxiety is the alarm, what is the house?
  • You have been breathing your whole life without trying. Why do you believe stopping is in danger of happening?
  • What is the space between one breath and the next?
  • If you were already calm, what would you notice that you cannot see now?
  • The tree bends in the storm and does not break. Which part of you is the root?
  • You are running from something that lives inside you. How far have you gotten?
  • The river does not hurry and yet it reaches the sea. Where are you hurrying?
  • You carry your past in both hands and wonder why your arms are tired.
  • What is the difference between a thought and the thinker?
  • When the anxiety passes, where does it go? Where were you while it was here?
  • You want to fix yourself. But what if nothing is broken?
  • The silence is not empty. What is in it?
  • You have survived every hard thing that has ever happened to you. What is the evidence that this one is different?
  • What were you before you learned to be afraid?
  • The harder you try to relax, the further away it gets. What would you do if you stopped trying?
  • When you say "I am anxious," who is the "I" watching the anxiety?
  • A door only works if there is something on the other side. What is your anxiety a door to?
Classic Zen Koans

These are traditional teaching koans. Use as-is or as a starting point for content.

  • What was your face before your parents were born? — Huineng
  • What is the sound of one hand clapping? — Hakuin
  • If you meet the Buddha on the road, kill him. — Linji
  • Does a dog have Buddha nature? — Zhaozhou — answer: Mu
  • What is your original home?
  • Before thinking, what are you?
  • Where do you go when you die? Where were you before you were born?
  • The whole world is medicine. What is the self?
  • When you are hungry, eat. When you are tired, sleep. Why is this so difficult?
  • Not knowing is most intimate.
Taoist Contemplations

Not traditional koans, but contemplative questions drawn from Taoist thought.

  • The Tao that can be named is not the eternal Tao. So what have you been naming?
  • What would Wu Wei look like in your life tomorrow morning?
  • The valley is empty. The valley is useful. What are you making room for?
  • Water does not fight the rock. It waits. What have you been fighting that might yield to patience?
  • Lao Tzu said: "A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath your feet." Where are your feet right now?
  • The sage does not show off and therefore shines. What are you trying to prove, and to whom?
  • What would it look like to do less, and mean it?
